How to Use This Site

This website is designed to help you understand and assess flood vulnerabilities using advanced fuzzy geospatial techniques. Below, you’ll find step-by-step instructions on how to navigate the site and utilize the available tools effectively.

  • Navigation: Use the menu options located at the top of the page to access different sections of the website such as Home, About, and Help.
  • Interacting with Maps: Click on the map area to view detailed information about flood risks in specific regions. Use the zoom tools to closely inspect different areas.
  • Selecting Parameters: Use the drop-down menus to select specific parameters that influence flood vulnerability. This will update the maps and provide a tailored analysis based on the chosen criteria.
  • Viewing Results: After selecting your parameters, press the 'Calculate' button to generate updated vulnerability maps. Results will be displayed directly beneath the map area.
  • Downloading Data: You can download the results of your analyses by clicking the 'Download' button located at the bottom of the results area.
